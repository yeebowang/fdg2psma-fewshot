\begin{abstract}
Under clinical concept drift from FDG to PSMA PET/CT, self-supervised pretraining
on source-domain volumes yields representations that adapt to the target tracer with
far fewer labeled cases than training from scratch---but this advantage is
\emph{shot-dependent} and collapses in the extreme low-shot / zero-shot regime unless
the adaptation protocol is matched to the drift.
We instantiate this claim with an aligned FDG$\rightarrow$PSMA benchmark that freezes a
shared FDG stage and varies only the PSMA label budget
($N\in\{50,10,5,0\}$), reporting Dice together with voxel-level FP/FN.
Across fifteen method variants, PET/CT MAE SSL leads at moderate budgets
(\texttt{fs50}/\texttt{fs10}), yet the SSL--scratch gap shrinks to noise by
\texttt{fs5}/\texttt{fs0}, where competing recipes can overtake---highlighting
\emph{when} representation learning does and does not buy robustness to clinical
concept drift.\footnote{ICLR workshop draft; numbers are live board snapshots.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

We use \emph{clinical concept drift} for tracer change in oncology PET/CT: not only
covariate shift of images, but a change in what ``lesion'' looks like (uptake
pattern, organ background) and how labels distribute (size, prevalence,
empty-GT rate) when moving from FDG to PSMA \citep{yixinchen2025hitchhiker}.
This differs from classical temporal concept drift; the inducing factor is a
change of clinical measurement concept (tracer), not calendar time.

Self-supervised / foundation-style pretraining promises rapid adaptation when
target labels are scarce \citep{he2022mae,tang2022self,mae_petct_arxiv}, while
nnU-Net-style supervised toolkits remain strong baselines \citep{isensee2021nnu}.
Work on normative representation learning further stresses that clinical findings
are rare and imperfectly observed, so average metrics on frequent phenotypes can
mislead \citep{bercea2025normative,bercea2025nova}.

\paragraph{Core claim.}
Source-domain SSL can reduce the labeled PSMA budget needed for competitive
segmentation under FDG$\rightarrow$PSMA drift, but the gain is concentrated at
\emph{moderate} few-shot budgets and is not automatic at near-zero labels.

\paragraph{Testable sub-claims.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Drift.} Cross-tracer lesion segmentation induces concept drift in
    lesion appearance and label statistics, beyond covariate shift alone.
    \item \textbf{Representation helps.} Under a shared FDG$\rightarrow$PSMA protocol,
    SSL initializations (e.g., MAE/MIM/dual-encoder) improve few-shot PSMA adaptation
    over scratch at moderate $N$ (here, especially $N{=}50$).
    \item \textbf{When it fails.} Gains shrink or reverse as labeled PSMA shots
    decrease; foundation-style or retrieval baselines may fail once the clinical
    concept has drifted.
    \item \textbf{Evaluation.} Fair comparison requires an aligned protocol
    (same FDG stage $\rightarrow$ fs50/10/5/0) and metrics that separately report
    Dice, FP, and FN under long-tailed empty/positive cases.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Aligned experimental protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Data.}
We use a stratified PET/CT cohort with tracer-aware roles (PSMA/FDG $\times$
small/large/negative lesion burden) and a fixed 70/10/20 train/val/test split seed.
PSMA few-shot subsets ($N=50,10,5$) are drawn from the PSMA train pool; PSMA
\texttt{fs0} evaluates the shared FDG checkpoint zero-shot on PSMA TEST;
\texttt{fc70\%} uses $70\%$ of PSMA train as a high-label reference; FDG TEST
probes source-domain retention.

\paragraph{Shared FDG stage.}
Every non-retrieval method first trains (or fine-tunes) on the same FDG supervised
split with matched global batch size and a common epoch budget policy for the board.
The resulting FDG checkpoint initializes PSMA adaptation unless the method is
training-free.

\paragraph{PSMA adaptation.}
Few-shot fine-tuning uses small labeled PSMA sets with online validation and
TEST evaluation aggregated over nine TEST folds (means in the main text; per-fold
grids omitted). Metrics:
\[
\mathrm{Dice}=\frac{2\mathrm{TP}}{2\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FP}+\mathrm{FN}},\quad
\mathrm{FP\%}=\frac{\mathrm{FP}}{\mathrm{Neg}},\quad
\mathrm{FN\%}=\frac{\mathrm{FN}}{\mathrm{Pos}},
\]
with Dice excluding empty-GT cases and FP/FN as voxel micro-averages.

\paragraph{Methods.}
We compare scratch vs.\ pretrained siblings when available: PET/CT MAE SSL
\citep{mae_petct_arxiv}, Swin SSL \citep{tang2022self}, nnU-Net/MIM
\citep{isensee2021nnu}, DpDNet \citep{dpdnet2025}, SegAnyPET
\citep{seganypet2025}, prototype retrieval, and AutoPET~V entries
(BIRTH / YixinChen).

\paragraph{Moderate few-shot: SSL buys rapid adaptation.}
At PSMA \texttt{fs50}, PET/CT MAE SSL reaches 53.0 Dice vs.\ 48.1 for its scratch
twin ($+4.9$\,pp) and leads completed methods (Table~\ref{tab:dice}). The SSL edge
remains positive but smaller at \texttt{fs10} ($+1.1$\,pp). This supports the
``rapid adaptation'' half of the claim under a matched FDG stage.

\paragraph{Extreme scarcity: the advantage collapses.}
By \texttt{fs5}, the MAE SSL--scratch gap flips slightly ($-0.6$\,pp), and DpDNet
scratch leads Dice (41.2). At \texttt{fs0}, most learned segmentors sit in a low
band ($\sim$11--19); SegAnyPET's higher zero-shot Dice coexists with severe FN on
few-shot columns. Retrieval does not transfer under this drift. Gains from SSL are
therefore \emph{shot-dependent}, not unconditional.

\paragraph{Failures are FN-dominated.}
Board FP rates are typically $\ll 1\%$, while FN often exceeds $30$--$70\%$ as $N$
decreases---Dice alone hides under-segmentation of sparse PSMA disease.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Matching objectives to clinical concept drift means: (i)~pretrain for transferable
anatomical--metabolic structure on the source tracer; (ii)~adapt with a protocol
whose label budget is stated explicitly; (iii)~evaluate with FP/FN under
long-tailed empty/positive cases, not only mean Dice
\citep{bercea2025normative,bercea2025nova}.
Our board is designed so the core claim is falsifiable: if SSL never helped at
\texttt{fs50}/\texttt{fs10}, or continued to dominate at \texttt{fs5}/\texttt{fs0},
the shot-dependent statement would be revised accordingly.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Under FDG$\rightarrow$PSMA clinical concept drift, source-domain self-supervised
representations accelerate PSMA adaptation at moderate few-shot budgets, but the benefit is
not automatic in the extreme low-/zero-shot regime. Fair, aligned protocols and
FN-aware metrics are required to locate that boundary. We will freeze incomplete
competition rows and add fold-wise intervals for the camera-ready.
